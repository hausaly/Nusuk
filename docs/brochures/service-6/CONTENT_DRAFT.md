# 2. WHY THIS MATTERS

A package is not a price list. It is a promise you must deliver to every pilgrim, in a different country, on a fixed calendar.

- **Pricing without costing loses money.** Many operators set prices by copying competitors. One missing line (transfers, ziyarah transport, food allowance, yellow card, domestic travel) turns a "full" group into a loss.
- **Itineraries that look good on paper fail on the ground.** Road times (Makkah → Madinah about 6 hours, Madinah → Jeddah about 5 hours) decide check-in times, rest and prayer schedules.
- **The Umrah season is short and rule-bound.** Visa issue, pilgrim exit and Ramadan/Hajj windows decide when you can sell and when you can fly.
- **NUSUK MASAR requires a package that is operationally ready**, not only commercially attractive. **[CONFIRM exact MASAR package/contract requirements wording]**

# 4. WHAT A COMPLETE PACKAGE INCLUDES

**Basic services (per pilgrim)**

- Round-trip flights (group tickets)
- Umrah visa
- Airport ↔ hotel transfers; Makkah ↔ Madinah transfer; ziyarah transport
- Makkah and Madinah hotels (room type per night)

**End-to-end mutawwif service** — your own guide travels with the group: travel package, food allowance, yellow card, domestic transport (for example Biu–Kano / Kano–Biu).

**Saudi-based mutawwif service** — a ground guide in the Kingdom: hotel and transport package, food and transport allowance, service payment.

**Additional services** — meals (breakfast/lunch/dinner), SIM and connectivity, market shopping assistance, medical check-up, laundry.

**Ziyarah** — Madinah: Baqi' cemetery, Masjid Quba, Masjid Qiblatain, museum, Masjid Ghamama. Makkah: Jabal al-Nour, Cave of Hira, Clock Tower, Masjid Taneem, Exhibition of the Two Holy Mosques.
