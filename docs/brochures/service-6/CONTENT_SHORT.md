# THE HARD TRUTH

**Most Umrah packages fail before the first pilgrim flies.**

- **Priced by guesswork** — one forgotten line (ziyarah coach, food allowance, yellow card, domestic travel) and a full group still loses money.
- **Built on paper, broken on the road** — Madinah → Makkah is ±6 hours. Ignore it and check-in, rest and prayer plans collapse.
- **Sold in the wrong month** — visa close, pilgrim exit, Ramadan and Hajj decide when you may sell and fly.
- **Not MASAR-ready** — a good brochure is not an operational package. **[CONFIRM wording]**

# WE FIX THAT IN 5 MOVES

1. **Design** — tier, group size, 14-day split (e.g. 10 Makkah + 4 Madinah).
2. **Source** — hotels, group flights, visa, transfers, ziyarah transport.
3. **Plan** — day-by-day itinerary, both routings, mutawwif checklist.
4. **Price** — cost per pilgrim, group rate, margin, break-even group size.
5. **Launch** — operational calendar + NUSUK MASAR readiness checklist.
